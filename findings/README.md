# findings

Findings sorted by evidence label. Nothing here is a conclusion without
evidence — each entry cites the `raw-data/` it rests on.

- `verified-facts/` — facts verified from primary evidence.
- `official-claims/` — claims from official Rayls sources, recorded as claims.
- `reconstructions/` — results rebuilt by combining observable data.
- `inferences/` — inferences drawn from evidence, with what could prove them wrong.
- `unknowns/` — questions that could not be answered with available evidence.

Direct observations are recorded in `raw-data/` and referenced from the
finding they support.
